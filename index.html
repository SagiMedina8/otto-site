איפה זה רץ

otto remembers for you.

Tasks, meetings that get transcribed and summarized on their own, documents and decisions, in one place, on Mac and iPhone, in Hebrew and English.

A meeting that becomes a summary

Record it; otto transcribes, summarizes, and reminds you what was left open last time.

Built around who you are

Product manager, consultant, teacher, researcher, or simply tidy: otto opens with the parts and the names that fit you, and everything can change.

Your data stays yours

Export everything with one tap, delete the account from inside the app, and terms written for people.

otto is on TestFlight for Mac and iPhone. You sign in with Apple; the account and the data live in the cloud in Europe. Anyone who wants to can run a private server and point the app at it.
